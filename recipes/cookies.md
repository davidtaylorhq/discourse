# Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

## Ingredients

- 225g (1 cup) butter, softened
- 200g brown sugar
- 100g white sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 340g (2¾ cups) flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 300g chocolate chips

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F).
2. Cream butter and sugars until fluffy.
3. Beat in eggs and vanilla.
4. Mix in flour, baking soda, and salt until just combined.
5. Fold in chocolate chips.
6. Drop spoonfuls onto a lined baking sheet.
7. Bake 9–11 minutes until golden at the edges.
8. Cool on the tray for a few minutes before moving to a rack.
