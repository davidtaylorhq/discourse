# Fudgy Brownies

## Ingredients

- 170g (¾ cup) butter
- 200g dark chocolate, chopped
- 300g sugar
- 3 eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 100g flour
- 30g cocoa powder
- ½ tsp salt

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 175°C (350°F), line a 20x20cm (8x8in) pan.
2. Melt butter and chocolate together (double boiler or short microwave bursts), let cool slightly.
3. Whisk in sugar, then eggs one at a time, then vanilla.
4. Fold in flour, cocoa, and salt until just combined — don't overmix.
5. Pour into pan, bake 25–30 minutes until a toothpick comes out with a few moist crumbs.
6. Cool completely before cutting.
