# Fluffy Pancakes

## Ingredients

- 250g (2 cups) flour
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 2 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp salt
- 2 eggs
- 375ml (1½ cups) milk
- 50g (¼ cup) butter, melted

## Method

1. Whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
2. In a separate bowl, beat eggs, then whisk in milk and melted butter.
3. Pour wet ingredients into dry and stir until just combined (a few lumps are fine).
4. Heat a lightly greased pan or griddle over medium heat.
5. Pour ¼ cup of batter per pancake, cook until bubbles form on top (2–3 minutes).
6. Flip and cook until golden, another 1–2 minutes.
7. Serve warm with syrup, fruit, or your topping of choice.
